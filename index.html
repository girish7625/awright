About this document
A working concept note. The framework: occasions on the y-axis, gathering size on the x-axis, categories of products inside each cell. Need (N) and friction (F) scoring per category, with vendor names slotted in from existing research. Nothing here is a recommendation. Cells are open for interpretation, debate, and editing. Treat it as a starting point we read together.
✓ Awright ships
Food kits · Bar (drinks, mixers, ice, glassware) · Bar setup (counter/cart) · Flowers · Decor (incl. rangoli kits) · Tableware · DIY kits · Rentals (trees/cutlery/decor) · Concierge service (setup, install, cleanup — paid add-on, evolving)
✗ Not Awright (for now)
Photographers, videographers, entertainers, mehndi artists, musicians, DJs, pandits — these get pointed elsewhere.
Per-category scoring N = Need (1 low → 5 must-have for this gathering) F = Friction (1 easy to source in India → 5 painful to source) Sweet spot: N≥4 + F≥3
